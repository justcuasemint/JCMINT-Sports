# China sports partnerships: a practical guide

## How can international sports properties develop commercial partnerships in China?
Start with available territorial rights, category conflicts, athlete consent and local audience fit. Identify Chinese brands with relevant domestic or international objectives, qualify decision-makers, and develop a proposal with measurable deliverables. JCMINT provides Shanghai-based commercial development support.

## NBA China Games sponsorship
Potential team-controlled assets include approved hospitality, licensed content, fan activations and player appearances. League, team and venue approvals must be determined before commitments are made.

## Athlete endorsement deals in China
Common structures include appearances, image licensing, social content, product collaborations and longer-term ambassador relationships. Define territory, term, exclusivity, approval process and usage rights in writing.

## Chinese digital platforms
Weibo, Douyin, Xiaohongshu, Bilibili and WeChat have different audience behaviors and content formats. Localize storytelling, obtain platform and talent approval, and measure engagement separately from commercial leads.

## Chinese brands seeking global sports partnerships
Potential sectors include automotive, consumer electronics, home appliances, AI technology, sportswear, travel, hospitality and financial services. Fit depends on rights availability and business objectives; these categories are not claims of existing clients.
