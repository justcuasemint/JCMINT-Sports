# Chinese brands and global sports | 中国品牌出海体育营销
Start with target markets and customer objectives, not celebrity follower counts. Assess athlete relevance, local distribution, creative rights, activation budget and measurement. NBA athlete campaigns may combine localized content, retail experiences and community engagement. Separate contracted deliverables from earned media and platform-wide exposure.
## 中文
中国品牌出海应从目标市场与客户需求出发，而非单纯追求明星粉丝量。评估运动员匹配度、本地渠道、创意授权、执行预算与效果指标。NBA合作可结合本地化内容、零售体验及社区活动。应区分合同交付、自然传播与平台整体曝光。
