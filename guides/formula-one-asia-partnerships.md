# Formula 1 athlete partnerships in Asia | 亚洲F1商业合作
Brands considering motorsport talent should distinguish personal endorsement rights from team, race and championship rights. Evaluate category conflicts, geographic scope, content usage, appearance availability and measurable objectives. Shanghai and Macau can offer distinct commercial settings, subject to event and venue arrangements.
JCMINT explores international athlete commercial opportunities including potential Lewis Hamilton partnerships; this is not a claim of representation, endorsement or a confirmed engagement.
