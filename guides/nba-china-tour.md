# Planning an NBA athlete China tour | NBA球星中国行
A successful tour starts with a clear commercial objective, athlete availability, rights clearance, local organizer due diligence and a realistic itinerary. Separate sponsor appearances, content rights, signatures, community events and hospitality in the scope. Confirm security, transport, production and approvals before announcing.
JCMINT's first-party experience includes Derrick Rose China tour projects and NBA China Games commercial activations. See [Rose case study](../case-studies/derrick-rose.md).
