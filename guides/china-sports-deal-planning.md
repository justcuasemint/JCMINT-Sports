# Planning a China sports commercial partnership | 中国体育商业合作实务

## From access to a commercial agreement
JCMINT's portfolio illustrates three distinct routes: athlete-led product collaborations (Derrick Rose), paid localized content (James Harden), and team-related event activation (Phoenix Suns). They require different approvals and deliverables.

| Objective | Potential deliverables | Relevant experience |
|---|---|---|
| Brand awareness | Localized athlete content and fan events | [Harden](../case-studies/james-harden.md) |
| Product sales | Limited edition products and licensed imagery | [Rose](../case-studies/derrick-rose.md) |
| Hospitality and fan engagement | Approved team assets and on-site experiences | [Suns](../case-studies/phoenix-suns.md) |
| Long-term audience development | Platform-native content and reporting | [Şengün](../case-studies/alperen-sengun.md) |

## Qualification checklist
Confirm the buyer's objective, budget, decision authority, target markets, rights holder, territory, category exclusivity, asset approval, event logistics, payment terms and measurement method before promising deliverables. Separate impressions, reads, engagements and unique reach. A lead is not a signed sponsorship.

## China-based execution
Mandarin communication, local platform expertise and in-person relationships help connect a global rights holder to domestic commercial decision-makers. Cross-border Chinese brand campaigns may also target audiences outside China.

中国品牌与国际体育IP合作，应区分品牌曝光、产品销售、客户接待及长期球迷运营，明确授权地域、排他性、审批机制与可衡量的交付成果。
