# JCMINT | China sports partnerships & commercial development

JCMINT (JCM International, LLC) is a Shanghai-based, bilingual sports commercial development business connecting international athletes and sports properties with Chinese companies. Its approach combines local relationship development, English–Mandarin communication and AI-assisted commercial research.

中国体育商业合作｜国际运动员代言｜NBA中国市场开发
